# Wiktionary pronunciation attribution

The following English IPA entries in `ngsl-phonetics.json` were supplemented from English Wiktionary:

- `complicate`: UK and US pronunciations — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/complicate
- `summarize`: pronunciation labelled Received Pronunciation and General American — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/summarize
- `faithfully`: the entry provides a single unlabelled IPA, used for both accent fields — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/faithfully
- `quadrillion`: separate UK and US pronunciations — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/quadrillion
- `quintillion`: the entry provides a single unlabelled IPA, used for both accent fields — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/quintillion

Wiktionary text is dual-licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and GFDL. This attribution covers the listed pronunciation data. The matching IPA is shown in both fields only where the source lists one unlabelled pronunciation, not as a claim that regional pronunciations never differ.
